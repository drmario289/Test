Security Notice: SIM-Swapping Threat Disclosure

Mandatory risk priming disclosure presented prior to multi-factor setup.

Recent security analyses demonstrate that traditional SMS-based One-Time Passcodes (SMS-OTP) are vulnerable to SIM-swapping attacks. Attackers can socially engineer mobile network operators to port your phone number to an attacker-controlled SIM card, entirely intercepting your SMS verification codes without your physical device.

Coping & Self-Efficacy Recommendation:

To ensure absolute protection against network interception, security standards recommend migrating to resilient authenticators such as FIDO2 Passkeys or App-based TOTP (Google/Microsoft Authenticator). These methods cryptographically bind your credentials to your physical device, neutralizing SIM-swapping risks entirely.
